# Page chrome & the shell

Chrome is not content. Nav, toolbar, route header (the bar itself), rail and page footer are **exempt from both floors and the ceiling** — **a card's own header band is not chrome** — the Craft 3 depth floor, the Craft 5 texture floor (a single-line footer and a text-only rail item are correct, not textureless failures), the alpha-vs-token-swap rule, and the atmospheric ceiling. A condensing bar is the one surface where alpha + `backdrop-blur` is the correct construction, because it genuinely overlays scrolling content.

- **Nav form is decided by REGISTER, not taste.** Application register, or any bar carrying real load: **flush full-width** `sticky top-0 border-b border-border-dark bg-background`. Editorial with one full-bleed image at the top of the route: **flush, transparent, borderless, condensing on scroll** — the default there. A **detached floating pill** (`fixed inset-x-4 top-4 rounded-full`) only when imagery must bleed to all four viewport edges **and** the bar holds ≤5 items with no input — and only with a `VETO:` line naming both conditions.
- **Capacity threshold (hard):** the moment the bar carries any two of {search, filters, account, primary CTA}, floating is dead — go flush. A pill that wraps, scrolls horizontally, or hides items behind a `…` is a failed pill. Below `md` a floating nav collapses to flush (`max-md:inset-x-0 max-md:top-0 max-md:rounded-none`).
- **Scroll state: all three properties, flipped together off one boolean at one threshold** with `transition-all duration-300 ease-smooth` — height (`h-20`→`h-14`), edge (`border-transparent`→`border-b border-border-dark`), ground (transparent→`bg-background/85 backdrop-blur-sm`). Shrinking the logo, fading opacity, or swapping a shadow alone is not a state. **The condensed bar is the only licensed `backdrop-blur` in the shell.**
- **Fixed chrome takes the safe area** (`pt-[env(safe-area-inset-top)]`, bottom bars `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]`). A transparent-over-hero bar earns contrast from a scrim on the **image** (`bg-gradient-to-b from-overlay/60`), never a text-shadow on labels. The trigger keeps `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls`; the open panel traps focus and closes on Esc.
- **Header vs page title — never both.** The bar carries route identity at `text-xs font-semibold`; the one `text-h4` title lives in the content column. The bar's `border-b border-border-dark` IS the page grid's top rule — the first section opens with no top border and no top shadow; two horizontal rules within 24px of each other is a fail.
- **The rail is the dark ramp's primary owner:** `bg-sidebar border-r border-sidebar-border`, `w-60` expanded / `w-14` icon-only, items `text-sidebar-text` → `hover:bg-sidebar-hover` → active `bg-sidebar-active text-sidebar-text-active`. **Never dark rail + dark bar** (the 2016 app frame); at most one other dark surface per view.
- **Toolbars** live inside the content column under the title: `h-11 px-3 gap-2`, controls `text-xs`, icons `size-3`, `border-b border-border` — never a shadow. **Footer:** application = one `font-mono text-[10px] uppercase tracking-wide text-text-muted` line, `border-t border-border-dark`, `py-4`; editorial = ≤4 columns, `py-10`, links `text-xs` — never sitemap + newsletter + social + app badges.

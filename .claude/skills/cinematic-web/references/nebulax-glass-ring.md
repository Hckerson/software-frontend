## Metaphor

A single glass band twisted into a Möbius-like loop: a seamless, continuous layer with no inside or outside. It is transparent, so what sits behind it (the infrastructure) shows through, bent.

## What moves

- The ring rotates slowly; between the two frames its reflections and the refracted slats slide across the band.
- A soft light orb above it drifts.
- Copy is static in both frames: the hero carries all the motion, and the layout stays calm.

Editorial three-column split: headline left, object centre, stat column right. Pill CTA with an arrow disc.

## Art direction

Greyscale only: charcoal ground, white ink, grey labels. All colour and interest comes from light: the slats, the refraction, a single highlight orb. Large, light, tightly leaded headline; small grey body; stat numerals large and thin with tiny labels.

## Why it works

1. The glass needs something to refract, so the background slats are not decoration: they are what makes the material read.
2. Restraint in colour makes it feel expensive; the object is the only rendered thing.
3. The stat column balances the headline so the object sits in a true centre.

## Borrow / avoid

- Borrow: a transparent hero with a structured background to refract; greyscale with light as the only colour; editorial split with a stat column.
- Avoid: the invented stats (never ship made-up numbers), "Discover how it works" style scroll cue (banned in SKILL.md), the web3 copy.

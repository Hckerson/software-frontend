## Metaphor

Thousands of glowing violet strands that enter as a loose spray and converge into a single point: scattered data becoming one pipeline. The picture is the product claim.

## What moves

- **Moment 1:** strands loose and wide, focal point undefined. Headline absent; a single line types in near centre ("Nm…"), with a small eyebrow above it.
- **Moment 2:** strands have converged to a tight point right of centre, bright where they meet. The headline "Next-Generation Data Infrastructure" has appeared right of the focal point, with a sub-line and two CTAs.
- Copy arrives as the strands settle: the reveal is cued to the convergence, not to page load.

## Art direction

Near-black ground with a faint violet vignette. Colour lives only in the strands (violet to lavender, white-hot at the focal point). White headline in a light geometric sans, grey sub-line, tiny uppercase labels. Additive glow; no UI chrome glows.

## Why it works

1. The hero is a diagram of the value proposition, so no feature list is needed above the fold.
2. Asymmetry: the energy flows left to right and lands on the headline, so the eye is delivered to the copy.
3. One colour, lots of black: the strands feel luminous because nothing else competes.

## Borrow / avoid

- Borrow: convergence as a scroll-driven state change; copy cued to the settle; flow that points at the headline.
- Avoid: violet specifically (it is a reference colour, not a house default); the web3 copy; the template nav verbatim.
